# YATL — Independent Solana Wallet Validation (1 November 2026)

**Paper-only / read-only / no Helius calls / no VPS process changes.**

This is a reproducible public-chain audit of the three June-2026 historical
buying-pattern groups and the 26-wallet shortlist previously generated by
YATL public-wallet discovery (PR #191). The original training target was a
token **eventually reaching a 2x maximum price**, not a trader's realized
profit and not an achievable follower exit.

## A. Freshness of all 26 wallets

The official Solana public mainnet RPC was queried once per wallet with
getSignaturesForAddress (max 12 finalized signatures). No RPC errors occurred.

- 24 of 26 wallets, all historically in behavior group 1, showed their most
  recent confirmed signature in August 2026, none during September/October.
- 18 of these latest timestamps fell in 2026-08-28 18:10:34–18:10:52 UTC.
- The other six fell in 2026-08-27 13:18:39–13:18:53 UTC.
- The group-2 representative last showed a signature on 2026-10-31
  16:49:28 UTC.
- The group-3 representative last showed a signature on 2026-10-31
  16:49:34 UTC.

**Important:** Recent signatures, including these two from October, do not
automatically mean recent trading. An account can be touched by a transfer or
other program action. Solana's public RPC has no full historical retention or
service availability guarantee.

## B. Latest transactions of three representative wallets

One bounded, read-only job read 12 finalized signatures for each representative
and up to 12 after-June transaction payloads:

- Group 1: 12 sampled post-June transactions, 9 with SPL token balance changes,
  3 with no verified SPL owner balance change.
- Group 2: one after-June signature within latest 12; no verifiable owner SPL
  balance change in that transaction.
- Group 3: one after-June signature within latest 12; no verifiable owner SPL
  balance change in that transaction.
- No sampled simple token-vs-USDC/USDT opposite-balance flow was verified.
  This DOES NOT exclude SOL-based swaps or multi-leg routes.
- Exact unit quantity from SPL integer pre/post balance metadata is preserved
  with decimals and source owner; USD route prices and inventory cost basis
  are not recoverable from this bounded sample.

## C. Stronger coordination fingerprints: 6 representative wallets

A separate 12-request public Solana RPC probe checked the **LATEST**
transaction of four leading group-1 wallets and the group-2 and group-3
representatives. All six wallets were on-chain signers but none was the fee
payer in its sampled latest transaction. There were no identical latest
transaction signatures among them.

**Group 1**, four independently signed latest transactions:
- Same fee payer across the four:
  43ybD2JZWvxFGGyGtei9qP6PR5MbpTerUxoANaJ7QzoG
- Top-level SPL instructions involved **burnChecked** and **closeAccount**.
- These are burn/account closure operations; do not mislabel as a profitable
  token sell or normal trading decision.

**Groups 2 and 3**, two independently signed latest transactions:
- Same fee payer:
  DeBD2SxcE1TKji92CBxaY1NtJx6AJvH4PuTgVCYYGfqx
- Both called the Pump.fun AMM program
  pAMMBay6oceH9fJKBRHGP5D4bD4sWpmSwMn52FMfXEA
  and included a top-level system transfer instruction.
- Their transaction timestamps are 6 seconds apart.
- These two latest transactions show no verifiable SPL token amount change
  directly owned by the evaluated wallet in the normalized receipt.
- They may be routed DEX interactions or other program operations; the audit
  is NOT enough to infer an exact buy/sell or financial return.

**Interpretation:** Shared fee-payer, near-identical timing and overlapping
historical token selection are strongly suggestive of operational
coordination or shared infrastructure. They do **not** individually or
collectively prove shared legal/beneficial ownership, prohibited trading,
price manipulation or lack of trading skill. Only six latest transaction
fingerprints were inspected: the same fee-payer evidence cannot be
automatically attributed to all 26 wallets.

## Research decisions

1. Do not treat the June top-26 wallets as 26 independent proven profitable
   traders. At a minimum, stratify by observed token overlap and fee-payer
   cluster, and require new independent periods.
2. A token's historical forward maximum reaching 2x is NOT a trader exit.
   No actual realized source PnL, follower executable quote, or profitable
   copy trade was established.
3. Do not fit a new rank to these same June 2026 holdout observations:
   the dataset has already been explored repeatedly.
4. The next dataset should use RECENT wallet/token transactions with exact
   token amounts, source selection frozen before outcomes, and matched
   unobserved control token conditions.
5. Keep costs minimal. Official public RPC one-shot development requests
   succeeded, but are rate-limited and unsuitable for continuous production.
   Do not turn these jobs into live polling or evade API limits.
