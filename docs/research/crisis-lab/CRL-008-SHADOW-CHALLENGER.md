# CRL-008 — Shadow challenger and ablation track

Goal: accelerate future strategy research without contaminating active P10.

For any challenger:
- unique strategy/version/config digest;
- development data separated from blind holdout;
- crisis/control/random results reported separately;
- NO-TRADE and BUY-AND-HOLD comparisons;
- ablation tests for any new feature;
- costs, drawdown, sample size, robustness and survival all reported.

News/Macro/AI remain deferred until a point-in-time, auditable source and an
incremental OOS benefit can be demonstrated.

A challenger may only become eligible for a future forward-registration. Historical
performance never grants access to active P10 or P11.

Exit gate: accepted challenger protocol, not necessarily an accepted challenger.
