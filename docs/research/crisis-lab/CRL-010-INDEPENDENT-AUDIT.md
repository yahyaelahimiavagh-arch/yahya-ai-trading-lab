# CRL-010 — Independent final research audit

Goal: independently recompute the complete Crisis Lab evidence chain.

Audit must verify:
- event catalog identities and timestamps;
- data provenance and quality manifests;
- historical replay equality;
- control/baseline calculations;
- synthetic scenario outcomes;
- severity/random-window registries;
- challenger isolation;
- Survival Certificate reconstruction;
- no P10 mutation;
- no Live/trade/order/AI authority.

Final dispositions are research-only, for example:
- INSUFFICIENT_RESEARCH;
- ROBUSTNESS_GAPS_FOUND;
- RESEARCH_BASELINE_ACCEPTED.

None unlock P11.

Exit gate: independent recomputation + exact evidence index + explicit operator
acceptance.
