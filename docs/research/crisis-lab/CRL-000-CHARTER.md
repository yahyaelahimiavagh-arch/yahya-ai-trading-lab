# CRL-000 — Charter and isolation

Goal: freeze the research boundary before data collection.

Required invariants:
- RESEARCH ONLY.
- Active P10 candidate/gates/window/evidence remain byte-for-byte untouched.
- P11 remains locked.
- No TRADE permission, exchange-order endpoint, quantity authority or AI direct execution.
- Findings may only inform a future versioned research iteration.
- Development, holdout, random-window and synthetic evidence stay separate.
- Negative results are never deleted or relabeled.

Exit gate:
1. directory structure accepted;
2. research evidence taxonomy frozen;
3. P10 isolation checks documented;
4. event/data work may then begin.
