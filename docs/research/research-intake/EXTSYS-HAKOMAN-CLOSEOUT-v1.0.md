# EXTSYS — Hakoman Forensic Closeout v1.0

Date: 2026-10-02  
Subject: Hakoman / Hakan Sedefçi  
Research status: **HAKOMAN-FORENSIC-INCOMPLETE**  
Closeout classification: **REPEATED_MARKETING_PATTERN_ONLY**  
Evidence boundary: **CLOSED AT CURRENT PUBLIC EVIDENCE BOUNDARY**

## 1. What is sufficiently supported

Contemporary Turkish media described Hakoman in 2021–2022 as an automatic
AI-assisted buy/sell robot associated with Hakan Sedefçi. Publicity material
identified Sedefçi as Hakoman's manager/CEO and described the product as a
financial-services trading software project.

Promotional coverage also reported:
- approximately 16,000 users;
- a team described as 25 computer engineers and 20 software developers;
- overseas dealership/expansion interest;
- awards attributed to Sedefçi/Hakoman.

These are **promotional/platform claims**, not independently audited operating
or performance facts.

Public social posts indexed from Hakoman/Hakan Sedefçi accounts also advertised
that the bot operated in the user's own Binance account and used explicit
"no-loss" / "100% no-loss guarantee" language. Some posts described loss coverage
through free subscription extensions.

This is useful evidence about **marketing posture**, not proof that losses were
technically impossible or economically reimbursed.

## 2. What was not established

The forensic work did **not** recover enough reliable primary material to prove:

- an audited equity curve;
- complete trade history;
- realized/unrealized PnL reconciliation;
- maximum drawdown;
- leverage distribution;
- exact strategy logic;
- martingale behavior;
- grid behavior;
- averaging down;
- loss-deferral mechanics;
- liquidation events;
- source-code continuity with later Hakan Sedefçi systems.

Therefore no technical failure mechanism is assigned to Hakoman.

## 3. User complaints / operational reports

Public complaint material exists around Hakan Sedefçi and later trading/copy-
trading activity. One complaint references prior Hakoman/VIP membership. Other
complaints allege losses from later OKX copy trading.

These reports are retained only as **user-supplied allegations**. They do not
independently establish Hakoman's historical economics or algorithm.

## 4. Relationship to MR Crypto / Kings Bot AI

Organizational continuity around Hakan Sedefçi is supported.

Technical continuity is **not proven**.

The investigation therefore rejects the stronger hypothesis that Hakoman and
MR Crypto necessarily shared the same algorithm or risk architecture.

Final cross-generation classification:

**REPEATED_MARKETING_PATTERN_ONLY**

The recurring supported pattern is promotional framing around automated/AI
trading and unusually strong safety/performance language. That is not enough to
claim repeated technical behavior.

## 5. YATL lessons retained

- Never convert "AI", user count, awards, or "no-loss" language into strategy
  credibility.
- A no-loss promise is itself a reason to demand stronger downside/equity proof,
  not a reason to weaken risk tests.
- Strategy/version continuity must be provenance-bound; a later system cannot
  inherit performance credibility merely because the creator is the same.
- User complaints may motivate adversarial tests, but technical controls must be
  justified by observed or reproducible mechanisms.
- No Hakoman strategy should be added to YATL from the current evidence.
