## 1. Historical product/economic claims

Historical Orbit Network material marketed exposure to financial-market activity
including Forex/CFD/commodities/crypto and presented investment returns as linked
to the company's activities.

The research did **not** recover independently verifiable:
- trade logs;
- broker statements;
- audited exchange statements;
- complete deposit/withdrawal ledger;
- equity curve;
- drawdown history;
- exact trading strategy;
- source code;
- reconciled proof that investor distributions came from trading PnL.

Therefore Orbit Network's claimed trading edge remains **UNPROVEN**.

For YATL, this is the most important technical conclusion: there is no reliable
strategy artifact to copy, reproduce, or promote.

## 5. YATL lessons retained

### 5.1 Payouts are not strategy evidence
Regular distributions or successful withdrawals for some participants cannot
establish that returns came from a robust trading strategy. YATL requires
reconciled market evidence and economics.

### 5.2 Principal-return promises are not risk controls
Contract language or redemption history cannot replace marked equity, drawdown,
tail-loss, custody and liquidity analysis.

### 5.3 Registration != regulation
A registered company must never be treated as proof of authorization to manage
investments. Regulatory identity must be independently verified and bound to the
exact activity/entity.

### 5.4 Product transitions break performance lineage
A shift from trading/investment claims to tokens, NFTs, metaverse land or another
brand must not preserve prior performance credibility without an auditable bridge.

### 5.5 Preserve full economics
YATL should continue to require:
- realized + unrealized economics;
- deposit/withdrawal reconciliation when relevant;
- drawdown and tail-risk visibility;
- exact strategy/version identity;
- forward evidence bound to the candidate;
- preserved negative evidence.

### 5.6 Do not use Orbit as an alpha source
No sufficiently verified algorithm/trade history was recovered. Orbit Network is
useful as an **operational/provenance/risk-governance case study**, not as a
strategy candidate.
