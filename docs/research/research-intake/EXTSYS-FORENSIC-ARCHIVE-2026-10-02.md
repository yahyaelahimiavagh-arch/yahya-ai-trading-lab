## Cross-system lessons retained for YATL

1. **Marketing is not performance evidence.** Claims such as AI, high win rate,
   guaranteed/no-loss operation, user count, awards, or international expansion
   do not substitute for independently reconcilable trade/equity evidence.
2. **Payout history is not proof of trading edge.** Cash distributions can exist
   without a verifiable mapping to broker/exchange PnL.
3. **Company registration is not financial regulation.** Corporate existence,
   a website, awards, or exchange branding must not be treated as authorization
   to manage money or proof of economic soundness.
4. **Version continuity must be proven.** Relaunches, renames, platform changes,
   new tokens/products, or successor brands must not inherit earlier performance
   credibility without exact provenance.
5. **Closed-trade metrics can hide risk.** YATL must preserve marked equity/open
   losses, drawdown, payoff asymmetry, tail concentration, and forward evidence.
6. **Complaints are allegations unless independently corroborated.** They are
   useful for hypothesis generation and operational-risk discovery, not as
   standalone proof of a technical mechanism.
7. **Do not reverse-engineer alpha from insufficient evidence.** Neither Hakoman
   nor Orbit Network supplied a sufficiently verified strategy/trade record to
   justify candidate extraction.
